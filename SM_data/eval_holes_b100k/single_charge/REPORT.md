# Single-charge exclusion: error statistics

Models: `best_statedict_gauss_128_hole_ZZZ_b100k.pth` for Z = 1, …, 9 (architecture `FlexibleNN`, 2 hidden layers × 128,
Gaussian activation; inputs `[Z_ion, log10 E, one-hot(Theory_ID)]`, output `log10 σ`). Each model was trained for
100 000 epochs on H-target data from **CDW-EIS and CTMC** with projectile charge Z removed, and is evaluated here on
that unseen charge. Generated by `MODEL_SET=b100k python3 evaluate_holes.py`.

**Charge classification.** Training charges are {1, …, 9} \ {Z}. A held-out charge is *interpolation* if it lies
strictly inside the training charge range, otherwise *extrapolation*: Z = 2–8 → interpolation (7 models),
Z = 1 and Z = 9 → extrapolation (2 models).

**Test set.** 57 points per charge (37 CDW-EIS + 20 CTMC).

**"Valid" points.** CDW-EIS drops to ~1e-28 cm² below ~20 keV/u for high charges (outside its validity range). These
few points dominate linear relative errors, so metrics are also given without CDW-EIS at E < 50 keV/u (4 points per
charge removed, 53 points left).

## By theory (valid points)

- CDW-EIS is reproduced better than CTMC for the interior charges 5–8: median 0.5–0.9 % vs 2.0–5.4 %, MAE 0.003–0.008 vs
  0.014–0.026. CTMC data are noisier and cover a narrower energy range.
- For Z = 2–4 both theories have similar errors (2.7–6 % median). At Z = 2 CDW-EIS carries most of the negative bias
  (MBE −0.039 vs −0.015), and at Z = 3 both are overestimated (+0.030 / +0.015).
- For Z = 1 both theories are overestimated (CDW-EIS +0.43, CTMC +0.38 in log10).
- For Z = 9, CDW-EIS 4.5 % vs CTMC 8.2 % median.

## Observations

1. **Interpolation in the interior charges (Z = 5–8) is excellent**: MAE log10 ≤ 0.015 and median errors of 0.7–1.5 %,
   close to the in-sample training error (training-charge MAE log10 0.011–0.016 for these models).
2. **Error grows toward the low-Z edge.** Z = 2 is underestimated (MBE −0.030, σ ~7 % too low on average) and Z = 3
   overestimated (MBE +0.025); both stay around 5–6 % median error. Z = 4 is in between (2.7 %).
3. **Extrapolation to Z = 1 fails systematically**: σ is overestimated by a factor of ~2.6 (MBE +0.41), at all energies
   and for both theories.
4. **Extrapolation to Z = 9 works well** (median 5.4 %, MAE 0.028). The large raw errors come only from the CDW-EIS
   low-energy tail.
5. **Low energy (E ≲ 30 keV/u) is the hardest region for every charge**, because σ (CDW-EIS) changes by orders of
   magnitude there (see `signed_error_vs_energy.png`).
6. Model `444_b100k` has an anomalous in-sample linear error on its training charges (σ MRE 8666 %), caused entirely by
   the CDW-EIS low-energy tail; its log10 MAE (0.046) and its test error on Z = 4 are normal.

## Files

- `metrics_per_model.csv`: all training-script metrics (MAE, RMSE, MBE, MRE, MaxAE, MaxRE) on log10 σ, plus linear σ errors,
  for test (held-out charge), valid test and train charges.
- `summary_by_model*.csv`, `summary_by_model_and_theory.csv`, `summary_by_class*.csv`: aggregated tables.
- `model_statistics_by_class.csv`: descriptive statistics of the per-model metrics.
- `curves_per_excluded_charge.png`: NN σ(E) vs reference data for every excluded charge.
- `metrics_per_excluded_charge.png`: MAE, bias and relative error per charge.
- `signed_error_vs_energy.png`: median signed relative error vs energy.
- `parity.png`: predicted vs reference log10 σ.

Note: `train_*` metrics include the internal 25 % validation split used during training (`train_test_split` had no fixed
seed, so it cannot be reconstructed).
