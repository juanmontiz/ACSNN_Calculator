# Hole models without Rudd (100 000 epochs): overview

Evaluation of the 129 models `best_statedict_gauss_128_hole_XYZ_b100k.pth`, generated by `MODEL_SET=b100k python3 evaluate_holes.py`.
Detailed analyses: [`single_charge/REPORT.md`](single_charge/REPORT.md), [`two_charges/REPORT.md`](two_charges/REPORT.md),
[`three_charges/REPORT.md`](three_charges/REPORT.md).

## Models and training

- **Architecture.** `FlexibleNN`: input `[Z_ion, log10 E, one-hot(CDW-EIS, CTMC)]` (4 dims) → 128 → 128 → 1, Gaussian
  activation exp(−x²), dropout 0.01; output `log10 σ`; target H (Z_target = 1).
- **Data.** CDW-EIS (37 points per charge, 10–10⁵ keV/u) and CTMC (20 points per charge) for projectile charges Z = 1–9.
- **Holes.** Every set of 1, 2 or 3 distinct charges is removed from training: 9 + 36 + 84 = 129 models. The 3-digit name is
  padded with the lowest charge: (5) → `555`, (1,2) → `112`, (3,5,7) → `357`.
- **Training.** `train_ensemble` in `train_totals_NN_holes.py`: all models with the same data size are trained together on
  the GPU. Adam, lr 1e-4, batch 32, loss `small_mid_huge_loss` (MSE + MAE + mean relative error), 100 000 epochs, random
  25 % validation split; the weights with the lowest validation loss are kept.
- **Convergence** (loss histories `history_hole_XYZ_b100k.npz`). The best validation loss is reached at epoch ~73 000–78 000
  (median), and after epoch 90 000 for 30 % of the models; the best validation loss still improved by a median 7 % between
  epochs 50 000 and 100 000. The training loss printed during training (~0.14–0.18) is much higher than the validation loss
  (~0.02–0.05) because it is computed with dropout active: with 1 % dropout the loss on the training charges rises from
  ~0.012–0.026 to ~0.14–0.17. The network is very sensitive to dropping single hidden units.
- **In-sample fit.** MAE log10 on the training charges: median 0.0146 (IQR 0.012–0.019).

## Evaluation

- **Test set** of each model: the excluded charges (57 points per charge). **Valid points:** CDW-EIS with E < 50 keV/u
  removed, where CDW-EIS falls to ~1e-28 cm² outside its validity range (53 points per charge).
- **Charge classification:** an excluded charge is *interpolation* if it lies strictly inside the range of training charges,
  *extrapolation* otherwise; a model is *mixed* if it has both.

## Main findings

1. **Interior charges (4–8) are interpolated accurately** in every configuration: median errors of 2–3.4 %, no bias.
   The best models reach MAE ≈ 0.008–0.012 log10 (≈ 1 % median), close to the in-sample fit.
2. **The error depends on the local gap, not on how many charges are missing.** An isolated hole has the same error with 1, 2
   or 3 charges excluded (median per-case MAE ≈ 0.015); the error grows when consecutive charges are removed (gap 2–3) and
   above all when the charge falls outside the training range.
3. **The low-Z edge is the critical region.** Z = 2 is systematically underestimated when interpolated (MBE −0.06, ~13 %),
   and Z = 1 is always overestimated, by a factor of ~2 for d = 1 and much more for larger distances.
4. **The high-Z edge is benign.** Extrapolation to Z = 9 gives 7–9 % median errors for d = 1 and ~23 % for d = 2.
5. **The number of extrapolated charges is the best predictor of model quality**, and extrapolation errors are a positive
   global shift of σ(E) that grows with energy.
