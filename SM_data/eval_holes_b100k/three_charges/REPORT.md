# Three-charge exclusion: error analysis

Generated by `MODEL_SET=b100k python3 evaluate_three_charges.py`. Models: `best_statedict_gauss_128_hole_abc_b100k.pth`
with three distinct excluded charges a < b < c (architecture `FlexibleNN`: 2 hidden layers × 128, Gaussian activation;
inputs `[Z_ion, log10 E, one-hot(Theory_ID)]`; output `log10 σ`; target H). Each model was trained for 100 000 epochs on
CDW-EIS and CTMC data of the remaining 6 charges.

## Design

- **84 models** (all triples from {1, …, 9}) and **252 cases** = (model, evaluated excluded charge). Each case is evaluated
  on the 57 reference points of that charge (37 CDW-EIS + 20 CTMC).
- **Valid points.** Unless stated otherwise, metrics exclude CDW-EIS points with E < 50 keV/u (53 points left per case).
  There CDW-EIS falls to ~1e-28 cm², outside its validity range. The `*_all_points.csv` tables include those points.
- **Metrics.** All training-script metrics (MAE, RMSE, MBE, MRE, MaxAE, MaxRE on log10 σ) plus linear relative errors in σ
  (mean, median, P90, max, fraction of points below 5 % and 10 %).
- **Baselines.** The same evaluated charge in the single-charge model `zzz` and in the two-charge models that exclude z
  together with one of its two partners (mean of the two such models).

### Classification with respect to the charge

With three excluded charges, the missing charges around z can form runs of up to three consecutive values. Each case is
classified by the local structure of the training charges around z:

| Case class | Rule | Example | Cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| interp. gap 1 | z−1 and z+1 are training charges (isolated hole) | z = 5 in (2,5,8) | 105 |
| interp. gap 2 | one neighbour also excluded (two consecutive missing charges) | z = 5 in (2,5,6) | 60 |
| interp. gap 3 edge | z at the end of a run of three missing charges (d = 1) | z = 4 in (4,5,6) | 10 |
| interp. gap 3 centre | z in the middle of a run of three (z−1 and z+1 excluded, d = 2) | z = 5 in (4,5,6) | 5 |
| extrap. d = 1 / 2 / 3 | z outside the training range; d = distance to the nearest training charge | z = 1 in (1,4,7) / (1,2,7) / (1,2,3) | 56 / 14 / 2 |

Model classes: 35 interpolation (all three charges interpolated), 45 mixed, 4 extrapolation: (1,2,3), (1,2,9), (1,8,9),
(7,8,9). The tables also count the number of extrapolated charges per model (0–3).

**Statistical tests** (`statistical_tests.txt`):
- The case classes differ significantly (Kruskal–Wallis H = 125.7, p = 1e-24), and so do the model classes (H = 27.4, p = 1e-6).
- Interpolation vs extrapolation: Mann–Whitney p = 2e-26 (median MAE 0.019 vs 0.135).
- Gap 1 vs gap 2: p = 0.008 (median 0.015 vs 0.022). Gap 2 vs gap 3: p = 0.11 and gap-3 edge vs centre: p = 0.17, both not
  significant with only 15 gap-3 cases.
- The width of the local gap correlates with the error: Spearman ρ = 0.27 (p = 2e-4) over all interpolation cases, and
  ρ = 0.39 (p = 4e-7) for charges 3–8.
- For extrapolation, the error grows with the distance d: Spearman ρ = 0.46, p = 6e-5.

## 4. Effect of the number of excluded charges

Median per-case MAE (log10) for the same evaluated charge, with 1, 2 or 3 charges excluded:

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.412 | 0.286 | 0.347 |
| 2 | 0.033 | 0.045 | 0.078 |
| 3 | 0.029 | 0.018 | 0.030 |
| 4 | 0.019 | 0.015 | 0.014 |
| 5 | 0.008 | 0.012 | 0.015 |
| 6 | 0.012 | 0.013 | 0.013 |
| 7 | 0.009 | 0.013 | 0.017 |
| 8 | 0.015 | 0.016 | 0.027 |
| 9 | 0.028 | 0.045 | 0.047 |

By case class (median per-case MAE): isolated interpolation 0.0148 → 0.0145 → 0.0153 for 1 → 2 → 3 excluded charges, and
gap-2 interpolation 0.0149 → 0.0222 for 2 → 3.

- Compared with the single-charge model of the same charge, the three-charge error is a median 1.50 times larger, and 77 % of
  the cases are worse (Wilcoxon p = 9e-14).
- Compared with the two-charge models that share z and one partner, it is a median 1.21 times larger, and 63 % of the cases
  are worse (p = 2e-10).
- For isolated interpolation of charges 3–7, the error does **not** change with the number of excluded charges
  (Kruskal–Wallis p = 0.75, Spearman ρ = 0.03). Removing charges far from z costs nothing; the error grows only when the
  extra charges widen the local gap around z (gap 1 → gap 2 → gap 3) or push z outside the training range.
- Z = 2, 5, 7, 8 and 9 degrade steadily with n; Z = 1, 3 and 4 do not, because their single-charge models are comparatively
  poor. Each of those baselines comes from a single training run, so this comparison should be read with caution.

See `error_progression_1_2_3.png` and `three_vs_one_and_two.png`.

## 5. Energy dependence and bias

| Case class | E < 50 keV/u (MAE / median) | 50–500 keV/u | ≥ 500 keV/u |
|---|---|---|---|
| interp. gap 1 | 0.041 / 7.9 % | 0.024 / 3.3 % | 0.020 / 2.5 % |
| interp. gap 2 | 0.049 / 10.1 % | 0.030 / 4.1 % | 0.029 / 3.3 % |
| interp. gap 3 (edge / centre, MAE) | 0.051 / 0.052 | 0.033 / 0.030 | 0.041 / 0.051 |
| extrap. d = 1 | 0.143 / 21.1 % | 0.154 / 22.9 % | 0.169 / 18.3 % |
| extrap. d = 2 | 0.642 / 79 % | 0.493 / 73 % | 0.465 / 105 % |
| extrap. d = 3 (MAE) | 2.820 | 1.644 | 1.106 |

- **Interpolation.** The error is largest at low energy, where σ rises steeply, and smallest above 500 keV/u; the bias stays
  close to zero (|MBE| ≤ 0.04 in every band). Gap-3 cases do not improve at high energy as much as gap 1–2.
- **Extrapolation.** The bias is positive and grows with energy: for d = 1 the MBE goes from −0.01 below 50 keV/u to +0.17
  above 500 keV/u, and for d = 2 from +0.10 to +0.46. The NN overestimates σ and also gets the high-energy slope of σ(Z) wrong
  (see `error_vs_energy_by_case_class.png`, `curves_selected_triples.png`).
- **CDW-EIS vs CTMC.** CDW-EIS is reproduced better in interpolation (gap 1: median 2.4 % vs 4.6 %; gap 2: 3.4 % vs 5.1 %).
  In extrapolation both theories have similar errors (d = 1: 19.6 % vs 22.5 %).

## 6. Conclusions

1. **Interpolation in the interior charges stays reliable with three charges removed.** Charges 4–8 have median errors of
   2–9 %, even with three consecutive charges missing. The best models reach MAE ≈ 0.012 log10 (1.8–1.9 % median).
2. **The error grows with the width of the local gap, not with the number of excluded charges.** Gap 1 < gap 2 < gap 3, with
   median per-case MAE 0.015, 0.022 and 0.022–0.038, while isolated holes have the same error with 1, 2 or 3 charges excluded.
3. **The low-Z edge is the critical region.**
   - Z = 2 is systematically underestimated whenever it is interpolated.
   - Z = 3 degrades when 2 and 4 are also missing.
   - Z = 1 is always overestimated, by a factor of ~2 / 7 / 240 for d = 1 / 2 / 3.
4. **The high-Z edge is benign.** Z = 9 keeps median errors of 7–23 % for d ≤ 2, and 78 % even for d = 3.
5. **The number of extrapolated charges is the best single predictor of model quality.** The pooled MAE is
   0.032 / 0.079 / 0.176 / 0.452 for 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 extrapolated charges.
