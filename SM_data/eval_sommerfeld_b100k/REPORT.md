# CDW-EIS errors and the Sommerfeld parameter

Generated by `MODEL_SET=b100k python3 sommerfeld_analysis.py`. Theory: **CDW-EIS only**.

## Definition

CDW-EIS is a perturbative, high-velocity approximation. It is reliable when the Sommerfeld parameter
η = Z_P / v < 1, with v the projectile velocity in atomic units. With E in keV/u,
v = (E / E_au)^1/2, where E_au = m_u c² α² / 2 = 24.80 keV/u, so

> η < 1  ⇔  E > 24.80 Z_P² keV/u.

The CDW-EIS points are split into two regimes (`sommerfeld_regimes.png`, `sommerfeld_thresholds.csv`):

| Z_P | η = 1 at E [keV/u] | Points with η < 1 | Points with η ≥ 1 and E ≥ 50 keV/u |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 | 35 | 0 |
| 2 | 99 | 28 | 5 |
| 3 | 223 | 26 | 7 |
| 4 | 397 | 25 | 8 |
| 5 | 620 | 22 | 11 |
| 6 | 893 | 20 | 13 |
| 7 | 1215 | 18 | 15 |
| 8 | 1587 | 18 | 15 |
| 9 | 2009 | 17 | 16 |

- **Points left out.** CDW-EIS points with η ≥ 1 and E < 50 keV/u are not evaluated, as in the rest of the analysis.
- **Z_P = 1.** Because the η < 1 criterion replaces the fixed 50 keV/u cut, the η < 1 set of Z_P = 1 includes
  30 and 40 keV/u.
- **Models.** The ACSNN interpolator, all charges in training, is evaluated in-sample. The 129 hole models `_b100k`
  are evaluated on their excluded charges.

- The interpolator fits CDW-EIS equally well in both regimes: every point is within 10 %.

### Per case, same case evaluated in both regimes (`per_case_regime_comparison.csv`)

| Case class | Cases with both regimes | Median per-case MAE, η < 1 | Median per-case MAE, η ≥ 1 | Larger at η < 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| interp. gap 1 | 154 | 0.0109 | 0.0111 | 52 % |
| interp. gap 2 | 72 | 0.0130 | 0.0173 | 54 % |
| interp. gap 3 | 15 | 0.0305 | 0.0149 | 87 % |
| extrap. d = 1 | 45 (Z_P = 1 has only η < 1) | 0.037 | 0.033 | 64 % |
| extrap. d = 2 | 9 | 0.087 | 0.066 | 67 % |

1. **Interpolation across one or two missing charges is equally accurate in both regimes.** Isolated holes give
   0.011 vs 0.011; gaps of two give 0.013 vs 0.017. About half of the cases are larger at η < 1.
2. **Gap-3 cases are worse in the perturbative regime; extrapolation does not improve there.**
   - Gap-3 cases have twice the error at η < 1 (0.031 vs 0.015; 13 of 15 cases).
   - Extrapolations with points in both regimes differ less: 0.037 vs 0.033 for d = 1 (64 % larger at η < 1) and
     0.087 vs 0.066 for d = 2 (6 of 9 cases). Z_P = 1, the worst extrapolated charge, has only η < 1 points.
   - The signed error (`error_vs_sommerfeld.png`) shows that in extrapolation the network overestimates σ more and more
     as η decreases, by +15 % to +80 % for d = 1 and up to +600 % for d = 2.
   - This is consistent with the Z_P² analysis: the networks get the charge exponent wrong at high energy, flat toward
     Z_P = 1 and overshooting toward Z_P = 9.
3. **By charge** (pooled MAE, η < 1 vs η ≥ 1; `holes_by_charge_and_regime.csv`):
   - **Larger at η < 1:** Z_P = 3 (0.031 vs 0.018) and Z_P = 4 (0.018 vs 0.014).
   - **Z_P = 9:** similar MAE in both regimes, but overestimated by 12 % at η < 1 (MBE +0.050) and unbiased at η ≥ 1.
   - **Smaller at η < 1:** Z_P = 6–8 (e.g. Z_P = 8: 0.018 vs 0.024).
   - **Similar:** Z_P = 5.

### Z_P² baselines in the perturbative regime (`baselines_eta_lt1_by_case_class.csv`, `baselines_eta_lt1.png`)

Median per-case MAE on the η < 1 points. The baselines use only the CDW-EIS values of the training charges (see the
Z_P² analysis).

| Case class | NN | Born z²σ_p | Z² from nearest | Power law in Z | NN better than Born / power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| interp. gap 1 | 0.011 | 0.022 | 0.0002 | 0.0009 | 72 % / 0 % |
| interp. gap 2 | 0.013 | 0.022 | 0.0071 | 0.0018 | 60 % / 0 % |
| interp. gap 3 | 0.031 | 0.022 | 0.0056 | 0.0031 | 33 % / 0 % |
| extrap. d = 1 | 0.071 | 0.025 | 0.0078 | 0.0027 | 45 % / 0 % |
| extrap. d = 2 | 0.338 | 0.025 | 0.021 | 0.012 | 0 % / 0 % |
| extrap. d = 3 | 1.20 | 0.025 | 0.106 | 0.099 | 0 % / 0 % |

- **Simple scaling is almost exact for η < 1.** First-Born scaling from the proton alone is within ~5 % (MAE 0.022–0.025).
  A power law through the neighbouring charges is within 0.2–3 %, except for d = 3. In the perturbative regime the
  charge dependence of CDW-EIS is close to Z_P².
- **The networks never beat the power law in this regime** (0 % of the cases).
- **Pure Born scaling beats the networks** for gap 3 and for every extrapolation: d = 1 gives 0.025 vs 0.071 and
  d = 2 gives 0.025 vs 0.34.
- **Consequence.** Adding Z_P² scaling to the model, at least for η < 1, is the most direct way to fix the
  extrapolation in projectile charge.
